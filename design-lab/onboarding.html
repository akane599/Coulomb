Coulomb can see where your battery goes

It can see per-app drain, wakelocks and kernel wakeups. This phone does not expose its power rails, so energy per component comes from Android's power model .

Keep the monitor running

One UI may put Coulomb to sleep and leave gaps in its record. Add it to Never sleeping apps.

How to read the marks

Measured: counted by the hardware or the system
Modeled: from a model in Android or the battery
Estimated: Coulomb's estimate, with its range
Derived: worked out from other figures

Touch and hold any figure to see where its number comes from.
